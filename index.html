Hybrid Multi-Agent Trading System

Academic Research · SRM University · 2024 – 2026

Research question: can a team of specialised agents apply a momentum strategy with better risk control on NSE equities? Built a multi-agent pipeline in Python, PyTorch, Pandas, and Scikit-learn that implements the Minervini momentum strategy with agent-based risk allocation via LangChain, then evaluated it by backtesting on 5 years of OHLC data. Published in the IEEE AEEE Conference.
